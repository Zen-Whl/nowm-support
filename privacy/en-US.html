Privacy Policy

November 5, 2026

NowM is published through the Apple developer account of Beijing AtMoment Culture Co., Ltd. This policy describes version 1.0.

Information on your device

NowM stores the start and end times and duration of your quiet moments, plus any words or voice notes you choose to keep. Your writing is preserved as entered. The app needs no account and has no cloud sync. We cannot read these records from outside your device.

Collection and sharing

The app includes no ads, analytics, or tracking tools and does not send your records to our servers or third parties. It requests microphone access only when you choose to record. It does not request location, contacts, or health data. Recordings are neither transcribed nor uploaded.

System backups

If you enable iPhone system backups, Apple may back up app data according to your device settings. NowM does not operate a separate backup service.

Retention and deletion

Records remain on your device until you remove the app or erase device data. Uninstalling deletes local data on that device. Manage system backups in iOS settings.

Policy changes

If cloud sync or other data features are added, we will update this policy before enabling them.

Contact

For questions or requests, open a public support issue. A GitHub account is required, and GitHub’s Privacy Statement applies. Please do not post private notes or recordings.
