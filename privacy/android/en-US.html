NowM for Android Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026

NowM is a private journal published on Google Play by ATMOMENT (Beijing AtMoment Culture Co., Ltd.).

Information kept on your device

The app keeps session start and end times, duration, language preference, and any text or voice notes you choose to save in its private storage. The app works offline and needs no account.

Microphone

NowM requests microphone access when you choose to record a voice note. Recordings are saved on your device without transcription or upload. You can use the text features without microphone access or revoke it in Android settings.

Collection and sharing

NowM does not send your notes, recordings, or session information off your device. It has no cloud sync, ads, analytics, or tracking SDKs. The privacy link opens this website in your browser.

Retention and deletion

Records remain in the app until you clear its storage in Android settings or uninstall it. NowM excludes app records from Android backup and transfer. There is no account or server copy to delete.

Contact

Email nowm@atmoment.cn for questions about your privacy. NowM support
